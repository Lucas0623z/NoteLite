Local model graph modification (2026 NoteLite contributors): tf-divide-no-nan-coreml5-v1.
The upstream CoreML conversion replaced TensorFlow divide_no_nan with an unguarded real_div.
This repair restores exactly zero output when its denominator is exactly zero; all nonzero
division, learned weights, neural activation thresholds and other original operations remain unchanged.
No epsilon or amplitude threshold is introduced. The locked original operation and exact
reversible patch are documented in DIVIDE-NO-NAN-PATCH.json and apple/scripts/patch-notewise-coreml.py.
Original TF source: https://github.com/spotify/basic-pitch/blob/v0.4.0/basic_pitch/layers/signal.py#L157-L168
The original official model SHA256 is retained, and the patch can reconstruct its exact bytes offline.

The model's confidence values are neural activations, not calibrated correctness probabilities.
Its contour resolution is one third of a semitone; it does not alone establish precise intonation.
